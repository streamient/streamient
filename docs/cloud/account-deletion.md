# Delete your Streamient account

Only the account owner can request permanent deletion.

1. Open **Settings > Subscription** on web or desktop.
2. Select **Delete account**.
3. Confirm the account name and type **DELETE**.

Free accounts can delete directly. If the account has a paid subscription, cancel it through subscription management first. A cancellation scheduled for the end of the billing period must finish before deletion becomes available.

Access is revoked immediately after the request is recorded. Permanent cleanup continues in the background; an accepted request does not mean cleanup is already complete. Administrators can inspect progress and retry failed cleanup from the account screen.

Your access and role in other accounts remain. Logins that no longer belong to any account are removed. Billing history is retained.

Deletion removes the account's notes, memories, URLs, indexed pages, email data, exports, imported files, Git checkouts, and server-side Obsidian data. Original external Git repositories and local Obsidian vaults remain.

Existing accounts are never automatically deleted because they are Free or their trial expires.
